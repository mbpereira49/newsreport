Latent Space’s thesis is a neat reversal: ontologies are back because agents are messy. The excerpt says AI engineers are rediscovering them as a way to keep probabilistic agents inside deterministic boundaries. That is a very 2026 infrastructure problem: models can reason, call tools, and improvise, but production systems still need crisp definitions of entities, permissions, workflows, and constraints. The old Semantic Web dream was often mocked for being over-specified. Agentic systems may be making that over-specification feel practical, even necessary.

Why it matters: As AI agents move from demos into operations, “understanding” is not enough. Systems need shared schemas that make actions legible, auditable, and harder to misfire.
