Los Angeles schools move to put AI mostly off-limits

The district’s moratorium appears stricter than New York City’s newly announced plan, including tighter limits for older students.

Los Angeles is drawing a harder line on classroom AI. According to the report, the district has banned most student use of artificial intelligence, with a moratorium that appears to go beyond a similar plan announced by New York City. The key distinction is scope: Los Angeles is described as imposing strict limits even on older students, a group many school systems have treated as more capable of using AI with guardrails.

Why it matters: Schools are still choosing between prohibition, supervision and integration. Los Angeles is signaling that, at least for now, the risks of AI shortcuts and uneven classroom use outweigh the case for broad student access.
